# Chrome Web Store Screenshots Guide

The Chrome Web Store requires at least 1 screenshot (up to 5 recommended).
Each must be either **1280x800px** or **640x400px**, PNG or JPEG, no alpha
channel. Use 1280x800 for the crispest result.

Take these five, in this order, saved as `screenshot-1.png` through
`screenshot-5.png` in this folder.

## Screenshot 1 — Extension button on AliExpress product page

1. Open any real AliExpress product page (`aliexpress.com/item/...`).
2. Wait for the "🚀 Analyze with Launchory" button to appear (bottom-right,
   ~3s after page load).
3. Capture the full page or crop to a 1280x800 region that clearly shows
   the button in context against the real AliExpress product page.
4. Make sure the button is fully visible and not overlapped by AliExpress's
   own floating widgets.

## Screenshot 2 — Popup showing detected product

1. On that same AliExpress product page, click the Launchory toolbar icon.
2. Capture the popup: logo header, detected product title, and the
   "Analyze This Product →" button.
3. Since the popup itself is only 320px wide, composite it into a
   1280x800 canvas — e.g. centered on a plain dark background, or
   overlaid on a blurred screenshot of the AliExpress page behind it —
   rather than submitting a tiny 320px-wide image directly.

## Screenshot 3 — Launchory analyze page with full AI results

1. Click through from the extension to `launchory.io/analyze`.
2. Let the AI analysis finish loading.
3. Capture the full results: demand score ring, competition, price,
   ad angles, hooks, and the supplier/generate-ad action buttons.
4. Scroll/crop to fit the most information-dense, visually compelling
   part of the page into the 1280x800 frame.

## Screenshot 4 — Dashboard showing product feed

1. Log in to `launchory.io` and go to the main dashboard.
2. Capture the product feed grid with several product cards visible —
   ideally including a "Hot" trend badge and a few different niches so
   the variety of the catalog reads clearly.

## Screenshot 5 — Ad generator with generated creative

1. Open a product's AI analysis, scroll to the ad generator section.
2. Generate (or use a previously generated) ad creative image.
3. Capture the generated ad image alongside the format/style controls,
   so viewers can see both the input controls and the output quality.

## General tips

- Use a real, populated account — empty states and placeholder data
  read as unfinished/untrustworthy in store listings.
- Keep browser chrome (tabs, bookmarks bar) out of the frame, or crop
  it out — screenshots should focus on the product, not the browser.
- Avoid including any real customer/personal data in the screenshots.
- Export as PNG for the sharpest text rendering.
