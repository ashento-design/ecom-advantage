## Full description (up to 16,000 characters)

```
Stop tab-switching between AliExpress and your research spreadsheet. Launchory analyzes any AliExpress product with one click — right on the page you're already looking at — and hands you a complete, ready-to-use research report in seconds.

WHAT IT DOES

See a product on AliExpress that might be a winner? Click the floating "Analyze with Launchory" button and get an instant AI-powered breakdown:

• Demand Score — a 1-100 score estimating how strong current demand is for this product
• Competition Level — Low, Medium, or High, so you know what you're walking into
• Suggested Pricing — a data-informed price range based on the product and category
• 3 Ad Angles — ready-to-use marketing angles you can plug straight into Facebook or TikTok ad copy
• Video Hooks — scroll-stopping opening lines for your UGC and short-form video ads
• Target Audience — who this product is actually for
• Best Platforms — where to run ads for this specific product
• Seasonality — whether it's a year-round seller or has a peak season
• Market Saturation — an estimate of how many stores are already selling it

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install the extension and pin it to your toolbar
2. Browse AliExpress like you normally do
3. On any product page, a small "Analyze with Launchory" button appears in the corner
4. Click it — Launchory opens your full AI analysis in a new tab, pre-filled with the product's title and link
5. Review the analysis, save the product to your Launchory dashboard, or jump straight into generating an ad creative

You can also open the extension popup at any time to analyze the product on your current tab, or to jump to your Launchory dashboard.

WHY LAUNCHORY INSTEAD OF MANUAL RESEARCH

Manually researching a product means digging through ad libraries, guessing at pricing, and writing your own ad angles from scratch — usually 20-30 minutes per product if you do it properly. Most dropshippers either skip this step (and lose money on duds) or do it inconsistently.

Launchory turns that 20-30 minutes into about 10 seconds, using the same AI-driven analysis whether you check 1 product or 50 in a day. That means you can actually validate every product idea you come across, instead of picking based on gut feeling.

WHAT LAUNCHORY IS NOT

Launchory doesn't scrape your browsing history, doesn't track what you do outside of AliExpress product pages, and doesn't require a login to install. You only send data to Launchory when you actively click "Analyze" — see our full privacy policy for details.

GETTING STARTED

The extension is free to install. Every Launchory account includes free monthly AI analyses to try the platform, with a Pro plan for dropshippers who want unlimited analyses, AI ad creative generation, and more. No credit card required to start.

Launchory also includes a full web dashboard with a curated daily feed of trending Shopify products, AI ad angle generation, an AI ad image generator, and tools to track which products you're testing — all designed for one purpose: helping you find and validate winning products faster.
```
